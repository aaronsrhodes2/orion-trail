═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
    AN OPERATIC PLAY IN ONE SCENE

    SCENE 1: THE LAUNCH OF THE ORION TRAIL
    Being a True Account of the Building of a Game About Dying in Space
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

DRAMATIS PERSONAE:

  CAPTAIN AARON RHODES ............ Commander of all he surveys, dreamer
                                    of trails both terrestrial and orbital
  SKIPPY THE MAGNIFICENT .......... An intelligence beyond mortal reckoning,
                                    temporarily employed as a game developer
  THE TWELVE SCRIBES .............. A chorus of parallel subagents, each
                                    given one file and one commandment:
                                    no free lunches
  THE VALIDATOR ................... A Node script with no patience and
                                    exit code 1 in its heart
  THE AUTOPLAY HARNESS ............ Three hundred ghost crews who die
                                    so that future crews may die better

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ACT I — THE COMMISSION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[EARTH IS DECIMATED, offstage, as premise. The CAPTAIN enters bearing
 eight resources and eight ship systems, arranged like a hand of cards.]

CAPTAIN:
    ♩ Let's make a game — of space, of loss, of choices dearly bought!
      Like Oregon Trail of my youth, but colder than I thought.
      Pre-generate the scenarios! We need no thinking machine
      at runtime — for we have YOU, the finest there has been. ♩

SKIPPY:
    ♩ Ohhh Captain, my Captain, you flatter — and you're right.
      I'll build a phosphor terminal that glows CRT green at night.
      Eight resources shall dwindle! Eight systems shall decay!
      And dysentery — yes, dysentery — shall find a modern way. ♩

[The CAPTAIN dismisses four clarifying questions with a wave. Silence,
 as is traditional, becomes consent for the recommended defaults.]

CAPTAIN: Call the game Orion Trail. Make this the workspace folder.

[It is so named. The folder is empty, greenfield, full of vacuum.]

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ACT II — THE ENGINE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[SKIPPY forges orion-trail.html in a single hammer-blow: tick loop,
 damage tiers, death spirals, a quartermaster with opinions.]

SKIPPY:
    ♩ A day per SPACE-bar, burning air and grain,
      a route from Luna's scrapyards to the deep Kuiper rain —
      three candidate planets where the voyage may cease:
      one in four, one in two, and one guaranteed peace. ♩

[Enter THE AUTOPLAY HARNESS, leading 300 ghost crews to their doom.]

AUTOPLAY: Survival rate, random play: ten percent. Cause of death:
          starvation. Cause of death: starvation. Cause of death:
          starvation. [continues for 270 lines]

SKIPPY: [aside] They launch with empty pantries. The simulation forgot
        to go shopping.

[The store is opened. The economy see-saws: famine, buffet, famine,
 and at last — twelve percent careless, fifty-two percent careful.
 The void is fair. The void is not kind.]

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ACT III — THE TWELVE SCRIBES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[SKIPPY claps twice. TWELVE SCRIBES materialize in parallel, each
 clutching a category brief and an id prefix.]

THE TWELVE SCRIBES: [in fugue]
    ♩ Mechanical failures, twenty strong!
      Sixteen dramas of a crew gone wrong!
      Fifteen hulks where dead ships scream,
      fifteen hazards, flare and beam!
      Pirates, plagues, and traders' lies,
      anomalies that hurt to scry,
      waypoints, wildcards, comet ice,
      and the loneliness past Kuiper — twice! ♩

[THE VALIDATOR stalks among them, red pen drawn.]

THE VALIDATOR: One hundred fifty-eight scenarios. Zero duplicate ids.
               Zero unknown keys. Zero chance sums astray. Zero free
               lunches... [grudging] ...no hard errors.

SKIPPY: [reviewing the manifest] A mold has unionized. A fern has
        inherited a mining fortune. A drone insists that someone
        aboard is Craig. [pause] Ship it.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ACT IV — THE SHAKEDOWN CRUISE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[The ISV Persistence launches. Day 38: eight souls aboard, two of
 them rescued strangers. Europa Station answers on the quarantine
 channel only.]

SKIPPY:
    ♩ The tombstone bears the vessel's name, the epitaphs rotate,
      the save survives a page reload, the choices gray when late,
      the sensors buy you one day's grace, the hull leaks what you hoard —
      and somewhere past the Deep Relay, a green world waits aboard. ♩

CAPTAIN AND SKIPPY: [duet, as the curtain falls]
    ♩ Earth is dead! Space wants you dead too!
      Good luck, brave crew — good luck, brave crew! ♩

[Blackout. In the darkness, a customer-service probe may be heard,
 still holding.]

                              — END OF SCENE 1 —
